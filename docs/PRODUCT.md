## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are parents and carers evaluating a dance or theatre school for their child. They need to understand whether SJP is welcoming and credible, identify an age-appropriate class, see practical details, and take a low-risk first step.

Teenagers are an important secondary audience. They may discover SJP independently and need age-appropriate classes, visible progression, credible teaching, performance opportunities, and a clear sense of the people and atmosphere.

Existing families, adults seeking available classes, prospective private-lesson students, and external partners are supported without displacing the first-time family journey.

## Product Purpose

The SJP Theatre Arts website helps families discover the school, find a suitable class, book a trial, and progress towards registration.

The primary conversion journey is:

**Find a class → book a trial or enquire → register through the booking portal**

Success means a suitable visitor can move from interest to a matched class and completed enquiry or registration without having to understand SJP’s internal structure or hunt for practical information.

## Positioning

SJP combines professional dance and performing-arts training with the warmth and personal guidance of a family-focused Bromsgrove school.

It supports first-time students, recreational participation, growing performers, and more ambitious pathways without making students feel they must already fit a particular mould.

## Product Principles

1. Help visitors establish fit before asking them to register.
2. Organise discovery around the student’s age, interests, and practical availability.
3. Pair aspiration and performance with reassurance, belonging, and clear next steps.
4. Treat timetable, fees, trials, and first-visit guidance as core decision content.
5. End every major journey by answering: “Is this right for us, and what do we do next?”

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website must support parents, teenagers, beginners, different abilities, and students who may need additional support without implying that natural talent or prior experience is required.

Content and photography should avoid gendered assumptions, use meaningful image descriptions, and clearly explain practical expectations. Current studio accessibility and additional-needs processes remain to be confirmed before publication.
