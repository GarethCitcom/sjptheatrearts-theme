Jazz & Commercial

High-energy choreography, musicality and stage presence, in a class where individuality is encouraged.

DaysMonday & Saturday
TimesAsk us for times

Who it is for

Anyone from four upwards who wants to move to music they recognise. You do not need any previous training; roughly half of a beginner class has never danced before.

What happens in a class

1
Warm-up and conditioning

Ten minutes of mobility and strength work.

2
Technique across the floor

Turns, jumps and travelling steps, broken down.

3
Routine of the term

Often the piece they perform at the Christmas show.

What to wear

  • ·Leggings or shorts and a fitted top
  • ·Trainers or jazz shoes
  • ·Hair tied back and a named water bottle

Want to watch first?

Parents are welcome to sit in on a trial class. Just say so when you get in touch.

Fees are invoiced per term. If paying in one go is difficult, tell us and we will set up instalments.

Questions we get asked most

My child has never danced. Will they be behind?

No. Beginners join a class where other people are also learning it for the first time.

Is the atmosphere competitive?

Only if a student chooses it. The troupes compete; the rest of the school is recreational.

Can I stay and watch?

During a trial, yes. After that we run watching weeks so you can see progress.

My child has additional needs. What are the options?

Group classes, one-to-one lessons, or a mix. Get in touch and we will talk it through first.

Our story

A welcome from SJ

I trained at Laine Theatre Arts from the age of 18, on a personal scholarship from Betty Laine, studying dance across every genre alongside singing and acting.

None of that matters much on a Tuesday evening. What matters is that a seven-year-old who was hiding behind their mum in September is front and centre by December.
