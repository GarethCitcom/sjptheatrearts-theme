+ What happens in a class

01
Warm-up and conditioning

Ten minutes of mobility and strength so bodies are ready.

02
Technique across the floor

Turns, jumps and travelling steps, repeated until they land.

03
Routine of the term

A piece built up week by week, often for the Christmas show.

04
Performance practice

Running it full out, in groups. This is where the nerves go.

SJ · founder and principal

I trained at Laine Theatre Arts on a personal scholarship from Betty Laine, and have performed and choreographed across the Midlands.

None of that matters much on a Tuesday evening. What matters is that a seven-year-old hiding behind their mum in September is front and centre by December.
