Our story

A school where every child is known by name

SJP Theatre Arts (formerly Bromsgrove School of Dance) is led by SJ, who trained at Laine Theatre Arts on a personal scholarship from Betty Laine and has performed and choreographed across the Midlands and beyond.

That professional standard sits inside a school where beginners are genuinely welcome and nobody has to fit a mould to belong here.

Joining is three steps

Not sure which class fits? Leave the class blank and we will recommend one.

  1. 01
    Tell us about your child

    Age, what they are interested in, and whether they have danced before.

  2. 02
    We reply with class options

    Lottie will send suitable classes, times and the next available start date.

  3. 03
    Come and try it

    A free trial class, then register through the member portal when you are happy.

What to wear for a first class: comfortable clothes they can move in and bare feet or trainers. No uniform needed until you have decided to join.
